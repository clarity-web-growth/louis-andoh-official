Private Gold Execution Advisory

Structured refinement for capital-based traders.

This advisory operates in small cohort format (2–5 traders maximum). The objective is not signal distribution, but execution calibration, risk architecture refinement, and structural decision discipline.

Eligibility Standard

  • $5,000+ active trading capital
  • Defined execution model
  • Risk exposure capped at 1–2% per trade
  • Journaled trading history

Not Structured For

  • Signal dependency
  • High-frequency emotional scalping
  • Under-capitalized speculation
  • Undisciplined risk behavior


Cohort access is limited. Applications are reviewed selectively.

Two annual cohort cycles (January–March and July–September). Approved applicants outside enrollment windows are placed on priority waitlist.
